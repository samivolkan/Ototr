# OTOTR ERP + CRM Product Blueprint

## 1. Urun Amaci

OTOTR ERP + CRM, genel merkezin tum franchise agini tek merkezden yonetmesi icin tasarlanir.
Ana hedef, CEO'nun her subeyi, her adayi, her musteriyi ve her kritik karari veriyle gorebilmesidir.

## 4. Tamamlanan Urun Akislari

### 4.1 Sube Profil Akisi

Kaynak:
- Sube tablosu
- Finans paneli
- Is zekasi sube karsilastirmasi

Detay:
- Sube saglik skoru
- Ciro
- Royalty
- Net katkı
- Kapasite
- SLA
- Bekleme suresi
- Egitim tamamlanma
- Sikayet
- Raporlar
- Randevular
- Merkez aksiyon plani

### 4.2 Franchise Aday Degerlendirme

Kaynak:
- Franchise lead kanban
- Franchise buyume room

Detay:
- Finansal yeterlilik
- Karakter / disiplin
- Lokasyon gucu
- Marka uyumu
- Kapanis olasiligi
- Yatirim butcesi
- Gorusme notlari
- Riskler
- Sonraki aksiyonlar

### 4.3 Musteri 360

Kaynak:
- Randevu tablosu
- Musteri tablosu
- Rapor gecmisi

Detay:
- Musteri bilgisi
- Ziyaret sayisi
- NPS
- Sadakat puani
- Randevu gecmisi
- Rapor gecmisi
- WhatsApp takip akisları
- Tekrar satis hatirlatma

### 4.4 Is Zekasi Kokpiti

Kaynak:
- Sube metrikleri
- Pazarlama funnel
- Finans ve royalty verileri
- Hukuk risk dosyalari

Detay:
- Ag ciro trendi
- Sube bazli performans derinligi
- Yeni sehir oncelik skoru
- Funnel kayip noktasi
- CEO alarm paneli
- Tek tikla sube profiline inme

### 4.5 Hukuk ve Uyum

Kaynak:
- Franchise sozlesmeleri
- Rapor itirazlari
- KVKK / acik riza dosyalari
- Marka kullanim kontrolleri

Detay:
- Hukuki maruziyet
- Risk seviyesi
- Sorumlu ve termin
- Delil toplama durumu
- Bayi fesih / yenileme sureci
- Rapor dili ve kalite baglantisi

### 4.6 Operasyon ve Kalite

Kaynak:
- Canli ekspertiz kuyrugu
- Personel performansi
- Rapor kalite orneklemi
- Egitim tamamlama

Detay:
- Saatlik kapasite
- SLA ve gecikme riski
- SOP adimlari
- Hata orani
- Musteri teslim deneyimi
- Gizli musteri ve denetim aksiyonlari

### 4.7 Sikayet Cozum Merkezi

Kaynak:
- Web form
- Call center entry
- Branch manual entry
- WhatsApp complaint intake
- Google review complaint detection
- QR feedback form

Detay:
- Wrong report claim
- Staff behavior
- Delay / waiting time
- Pricing issue
- Refund request
- Technical dispute
- Cleanliness / branch condition
- Appointment problem
- Communication issue
- Other
- New, Assigned, Investigating, Waiting Customer, Resolved, Closed, Escalated HQ akisi
- First response time, resolution time, escalation breach ve reopened takibi

### 4.8 Google Reviews Reputation Engine

Kaynak:
- Sube resmi Google review linki
- Yeni yorumlar
- Rating degisimleri
- Negatif yorumlar
- Guncellenen / silinen yorumlar
- Sikayet dosyasi eslesmeleri

Detay:
- Name, phone, date, branch ve case reference ile review/complaint matching
- 1 yildizdan 4 yildiza veya 2 yildizdan 5 yildiza recovery KPI
- Review recovery count, average rating, response speed ve review growth ile branch positive score
- Rating threshold, negative spike ve pending response alarmlari

### 4.9 WhatsApp CRM System

Kaynak:
- Official WhatsApp API ready inbox
- HQ, branch, sales ve complaint ekipleri
- Telefon, plaka ve CRM kaydi eslesmeleri

Detay:
- Unknown number gelirse source = WhatsApp lead olusur
- New Inquiry, Quoted, Appointment Pending, Booked, Visited, Won, Lost pipeline
- Appointment reminder, inspection ready, review request, complaint follow-up ve win-back template'leri
- Closest branch, existing branch owner, complaint desk ve franchise sales smart routing
- First reply time, conversion, messages per lead, booking ve agent productivity KPI'lari

### 4.10 Executive Alert Intelligence

Kurallar:
- Branch rating drops below 4.2
- Complaint surge +30%
- WhatsApp response above SLA
- Revenue drop vs last month
- Royalty overdue
- Staff churn risk
- Report error spike

Dashboard:
- Complaints Today
- Avg Resolution Time
- Google Rating Network Avg
- Reviews Recovered
- WhatsApp Leads Today
- WhatsApp Conversion %
- SLA Breaches
- Reputation Risk Branches

Ana is mantigi:

```text
Trust = fast complaint solving + strong communication + high ratings + consistent branch quality
```

## 7. Sonraki Teknik Karar

Bu iskelet dogrulandiktan sonra production yapi icin onerilen stack:

- Frontend: React veya Next.js
- Backend: Node.js / NestJS veya Fastify
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Auth: JWT + role based access control
- File storage: S3 uyumlu obje depolama
- Realtime: WebSocket veya server-sent events
- Queue: BullMQ / Redis
